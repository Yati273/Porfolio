01 / OVERVIEW

Project objective

A hands-on electronic prototyping project involving the construction of a Bluetooth speaker.

Hardware & software

Audio circuit board, Bluetooth input adapter, two speakers, capacitors, resistors, integrated circuits, wiring and enclosure.

02 / IMPLEMENTATION

How the system works

  1. Circuit assembly

    The audio board contains the electronic components used for audio processing and amplification.

  2. Audio input

    The Bluetooth adapter provides an audio input to the circuit.

  3. Speaker connections

    Wires connect the circuit board to the two speakers.

  4. Enclosure assembly

    The circuit and speakers are mounted inside the enclosure.

Component identification, circuit assembly, wiring and practical electronic prototyping.
