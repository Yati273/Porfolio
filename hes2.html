01 / OVERVIEW

Project objective

The HES 1 application reimplemented using STM32 Nucleo and embedded C, with multi-channel ADC and DMA.

Explore the other microcontroller implementation →

Hardware & software

STM32 Nucleo, STM32CubeIDE, HAL, two ADC channels, eight LEDs, potentiometer, water sensor, 5 V relay and 9 V lamp (specified in workbook).

Select a photograph to enlarge it.

02 / IMPLEMENTATION

How the system works

  1. Acquire signals

    PA0 reads the potentiometer and PA1 reads the water sensor. ADC with circular DMA transfers readings into memory.

  2. Below 2.6 V

    The potentiometer controls the eight LEDs and the relay turns the lamp off.

  3. Above 2.6 V

    The water sensor controls the LED sequence and the relay turns the lamp on.

  4. Update outputs

    GPIO controls the LEDs and relay based on the readings.
